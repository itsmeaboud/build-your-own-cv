# build-your-own-cv
For curious minds who want to see what really happens under the hood

Ever looked at OpenCV and thought
"How can all this complicated stuff work in just a few lines?" 😲

That curiosity, the one that makes you want to take things apart and build them yourself — is why this repo exists.

Here, I build classical computer vision algorithms from scratch. This is not about speed or optimization. It’s about understanding the math, the logic, and the small building blocks that make modern computer vision work.

If you are starting your computer vision journey or just want to understand the foundations behind deep learning, you are in the right place. Step by step, we explore, implement, and visualize core computer vision methods.
